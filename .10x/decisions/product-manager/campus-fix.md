# Scope and success criteria - Campus Fix

Date: 2026-11-11

- **Users:** students (report, view, upvote, earn) and one admin (change status).
- **Must have (rubric core, 25 marks):** login, report, view others' reports, upvote once, admin status change through Reported > Acknowledged > In Progress > Resolved.
- **Report fields:** category, building, room, exact spot, title, description, impact, photo, status.
- **Out of scope on purpose:** students assigning who fixes a problem (the brief forbids it); push notifications; maps.
- **Extras that earn their place:** duplicate detection, photo, search and filters, status timeline with admin notes, admin numbers, community goal.
- **Success:** every "Excellent" row of the rubric has a feature and a check behind it (see plan section 1).
